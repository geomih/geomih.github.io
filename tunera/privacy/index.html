Privacy Policy for TunerA

Last updated: 2026-10-01

Summary

TunerA does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Audio from your microphone is analysed on your device, in memory, and is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.

What the extension accesses

Microphone

TunerA needs your microphone to detect the pitch of the sound around it. That is the entire purpose of the extension.

  • The microphone is only active while the tuner is running. It starts when you press Start Tuner and stops when you press Stop Tuner or close the tab.
  • Audio is read frame by frame into a short in-memory buffer (under a tenth of a second at a time), analysed for its fundamental frequency, and then overwritten by the next frame.
  • No audio is recorded, written to disk, or transmitted. There is no server to transmit it to.
  • Your browser asks for microphone permission separately and you can revoke it at any time in your browser's site settings.

Permissions

The extension requests no browser permissions and stores nothing.

What the extension does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking.
  • No cookies, advertising identifiers, or fingerprinting.
  • No network requests of any kind. The extension has no host permissions and contacts no servers. It works fully offline.
  • No access to your browsing history, the content of your tabs, bookmarks, or any website you visit.
  • No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties, because none is collected.

Data retention and deletion

No data is retained, so there is nothing to delete.

Children's privacy

TunerA collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date above will change.
